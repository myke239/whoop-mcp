    Choose your platform

    Authorize privately

    Run setup, login and verify locally. Your client secret and tokens stay in encrypted local storage.

    Bring WHOOP into the conversation

    Enable the Codex plugin or follow the remote ChatGPT guide. Phone support depends on your account and requires live verification.
